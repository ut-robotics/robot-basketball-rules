= Robot basketball rules

== Changelog

=== 2026

. Robot size limits changed from 350 mm diameter and height to 300 mm diameter and height.
. Competitors area (black carpet) around the playing area (orange carpet) has been removed.
. Reacting to referee signals is now mandatory and manual starting is not allowed.
. Robot will now receive a foul for moving the walls.
. Robot will now receive a foul for ramming and for keeping pushing the opponent.
. Minimum wall height changed from 100 mm to 40 mm.
. Walls can now be black, grey or wood colour in addition to being white.
. Bye in Swiss-system tournament does not reward victory any more.
Robot basketball management software defines the scoring system,
and it can change without changes in the rules.
